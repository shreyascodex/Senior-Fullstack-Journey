Chocolate Coffe

Chocolate coffee is a delicious and refreshing drink made by mixing **coffee, chocolate, milk, and sugar**. It has a rich chocolate flavor with the strong taste of coffee, making it perfect for coffee and chocolate lovers.

Ingredients

  • Coffe
  • Chocolate
  • Milk
  • Sugar

    • Instructions

    1. Boil the milk and let it warm.
    2. Mix coffee and sugar in a cup.
    3. Add chocolate and hot milk.
    4. Stir well and serve hot.
